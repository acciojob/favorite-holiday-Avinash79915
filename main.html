Manali


Manali is a beautiful hill station in Himachal Pradesh known for its snow-covered mountains, scenic valleys, adventure sports, and peaceful environment. It is one of the most popular tourist destinations in India.

  1. Solang Valley - Famous for paragliding and snow activities.
  2. Rohtang Pass - Known for its breathtaking mountain views.
  3. Hadimba Temple - A historic temple surrounded by cedar forests.
  4. Old Manali - Popular for its cafes and relaxed atmosphere.
  5. Beas River - Perfect place for river rafting and sightseeing.
  • Siddu - A traditional Himachali dish.
  • Trout Fish - Famous local seafood specialty.
  • Thukpa - A delicious Tibetan noodle soup.
  • Momos - Popular steamed dumplings.
  • Babru - A tasty Himachali snack.
